# TTB AI User Guide

## What the AI Tab Does
The AI tab lets you ask a question and receive exactly 3 concise answers. It works best with the app's broader prompt style: favorites, daily moments, memories, and conversation-starting questions that can become shareable entries. Results can be reviewed in recent history and saved to your account.

## How to Use
1. Open the `AI` tab.
2. Enter a question (3-200 chars).
3. Pick a category.
4. Submit and wait for 3 numbered responses.
5. Save or delete the query from history.

## Best Practices
- Ask one clear question per query.
- Add context for better suggestions.
- Prefer prompts that naturally lead to three answers.
- Use prompts that can turn into memories, short stories, or image-backed moments.
- Good examples include "What is your Top 3 Harry Potter Movie?" and "Bugun sana iyi gelen seyler nelerdi?".

## Limits
Usage is controlled per account and reset windows:
- Daily
- Weekly
- Monthly

If limits are reached, the UI disables submission until reset.

## Troubleshooting
- "API key missing/invalid": app configuration issue (developer side).
- "Rate limit": wait and retry.
- "Empty response": retry with a clearer question.
